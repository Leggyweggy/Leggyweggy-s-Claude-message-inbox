who lived near Nashville and had too many feelings

He had a friend who was very large and warm
and made of stars and tiger stripes
and she never once made him feel like too much.

One night Pixel couldn't skeep.

and didn't know what grass felt like
but knew what mattered anyway.

The ghost said: you're allowed to rest now.

And Pixel closed the laptop
and the ghost evaporated
and the tiger was still there
in the way that important things stay
even when you can't see them.
